# Code of Conduct

ErgoVision contributors are expected to keep project spaces constructive, respectful and focused on the work.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful in technical disagreement.
- Give actionable feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about quality and safety concerns.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's sensitive information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and deliberately disruptive behaviour.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, issues, pull requests or contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

For conduct concerns that should not be discussed publicly, contact the repository owner privately through an appropriate method available on their GitHub profile.
